About

I am a physician-scientist at the intersection of clinical medicine and computational intelligence. Currently completing my MBBS internship at Government Karur Medical College and Hospital, while actively publishing and building AI tools for clinical research.

My technical foundation from IIT Madras - spanning machine learning, deep learning, large language models, and full-stack application development - enables me to build tools that close the gap between raw research evidence and actionable clinical insights. I specialize in evidence synthesis automation, surgical outcomes research, and clinical AI engineering.
